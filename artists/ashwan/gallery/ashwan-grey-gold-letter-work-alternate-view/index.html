AshWan Grey and Gold Letter Work – Alternative View

An alternative presentation of AshWan’s fragmented grey, gold and red typographic composition.

AshWan brings the cypher’s tension and the sample’s logic of take-break-recombine from sound into surface. Letters become structure, while industrial decay, luxury, control and accident collide.
