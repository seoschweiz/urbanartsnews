About AshWan

AshWan (b.1971) is a Liverpool-born, Barcelona-based visual artist whose practice is rooted in graffiti and hip hop culture, running in parallel to a long career as a DJ and producer. That dual lineage shapes everything: the cypher’s tension, the sample’s logic of take-break-recombine, carried from sound into surface.

The work moves through letters, fragments, and shrapnel of urban language: text broken down until it becomes structure rather than sentence. Industrial decay sits next to gold chains: luxury and rust, control and accident, the same collision seen twice. Nothing fully legible. Nothing fully random.

AshWan holds an MFA from the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and is represented by Galerie Lesmeister in Germany. He is also co-founder and Director of BienCuadrado Galeria in Barcelona and has work in galleries and collections internationally.

About Ashwan and Urban Art in Barcelona

Ashwan’s work connects contemporary urban art with lettering, hip-hop culture and the creative reuse of everyday materials. Cardboard and other modest surfaces become spaces for layered compositions, rhythmic marks and graphic forms. This combination gives the work an informal street-level character while allowing it to move naturally between illustration, painting and mixed-media art. The Instagram works selected above offer a compact view of Ashwan’s visual practice in Barcelona. Strong lines, written elements and improvised surfaces create a direct link with the city’s urban culture. Rather than separating studio art from street influences, the work allows both worlds to overlap. Ashwan’s profile on Urban Arts News forms part of the wider Barcelona collection, connecting the artist with other independent creators and with the city’s evolving visual language.
