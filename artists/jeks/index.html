About JEKS

JEKS is the artist name of Brian Lewis, a self-taught graffiti artist and muralist from Greensboro, North Carolina. He is internationally recognised for large-format hyperrealistic portraits created with aerosol paint. About JEKS and Urban Art in Ontario, California

JEKS brings large-scale hyperrealistic portraiture to the Ontario, California collection on Urban Arts News. The artist, whose name is Brian Lewis, is based in Greensboro, North Carolina and began working with graffiti in the 1990s. His murals are distinguished by precise facial details, smooth gradients, strong contrast, saturated colour and an ability to preserve emotion at architectural scale. The three Instagram publications above place the selected works first and provide a direct introduction to his control of aerosol paint. JEKS has created murals across the United States and internationally, while his work in Downtown Ontario connects with the city’s expanding public-art identity and the O-Town Walls mural initiative. This profile links his official Instagram archive with the Ontario, California city page and the international gallery on UrbanArtsNews.com. It also supports discovery through topics such as hyperrealistic murals, portrait street art, American graffiti and contemporary public art.
