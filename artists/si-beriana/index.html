About Si Beriana

Si Beriana is a Barcelona street artist whose work is known for strong color, figurative imagery and a playful visual language that connects mural painting with contemporary urban culture.

Her works appear in the streets of Barcelona and often combine recognizable figures with surreal, dynamic or symbolic elements. The result is a visual style that is immediately accessible while still leaving space for interpretation.

Barcelona has become an important setting for Si Beriana's work. Pieces connected to the artist have been documented in areas such as Ciutat Vella, adding to the dense and constantly changing street-art landscape of the historic city centre.

Si Beriana's visual language often makes use of bold outlines, expressive characters and vibrant combinations of gold, orange, purple and other saturated tones. This gives her murals a strong presence within busy urban environments.

Figurative subjects play a major role in her work. Portraits, athletes, animals and symbolic elements can become part of compositions that mix contemporary culture with the spontaneous energy of street art.

One of the artist's documented Barcelona works features football iconography and the visual language associated with FC Barcelona. The club also previously highlighted a graffiti work created by Anna Patrusheva in honour of Pedri, connecting her practice with the city's football culture.

This connection between public art, popular culture and the city makes Si Beriana an interesting part of Barcelona's contemporary urban-art scene.

Her murals are not limited to decorative surfaces. They become part of the surrounding streets, architecture and everyday flow of the city, allowing people to encounter art outside traditional galleries and museums.

Urban Arts News presents selected Instagram posts and works by Si Beriana as part of an expanding archive of street artists and urban creators connected with Barcelona.

About Si Beriana and Urban Art in Barcelona

Si Beriana’s work is characterised by vivid colour, expressive figures and a strong graphic presence. Her characters bring personality and movement to walls and urban surfaces, creating images that remain immediately recognisable within busy public environments. Bold outlines and energetic colour combinations give the work a direct visual impact. The three selected Instagram posts above document different examples of this figurative approach. Together they show how character-based street art can interact with Barcelona’s architecture and everyday street life. Si Beriana’s work adds a colourful, human dimension to the city’s contemporary urban-art scene and demonstrates how murals can create encounters between an artist, a location and the people passing through it. This profile connects her selected works with the broader Urban Arts News archive of Barcelona artists.
