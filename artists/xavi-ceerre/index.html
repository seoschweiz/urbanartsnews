About Xavi Ceerre and Urban Art in Barcelona

Xavi Ceerre’s selected works form part of Barcelona’s broad and continually changing urban-art landscape. The pieces documented above show an independent visual practice shaped by the possibilities of public and contemporary creative spaces. Each post offers a different point of entry into the artist’s use of composition, surface and visual expression. Presented together, these works allow visitors to explore the artist before reading a fixed interpretation. That order is intentional: the images remain the centre of the profile, while this short text provides geographical and artistic context. Xavi Ceerre is included in the Urban Arts News Barcelona collection alongside artists working across street art, murals, mixed media and urban visual culture. The profile also creates a direct connection between the artist’s official Instagram archive and the growing city-based directory on UrbanArtsNews.com.
