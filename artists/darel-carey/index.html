About Darel Carey

Darel Carey is a Los Angeles-based visual artist born in Curaçao and raised in Colton, California. His practice focuses on optical and spatial perception through dimensional line drawings, murals, immersive tape installations, paintings and digital art.

About Darel Carey and Urban Art in Los Angeles

Darel Carey’s selected works introduce Los Angeles to the Urban Arts News city archive through optical art, dimensional lines and immersive visual perception. Born in Curaçao and raised in Colton, California, Carey later moved to Los Angeles and graduated from Otis College of Art and Design in 2016. His work uses repeated lines and carefully organised patterns to make flat surfaces appear to bend, expand or form three-dimensional spaces. The three Instagram publications above offer a focused view of this approach across contemporary visual art. Carey’s practice includes painted murals, tape installations, drawings and digital works, often transforming walls, rooms and architectural surfaces into perceptual environments. Natural patterns, emergence and the psychology of seeing are recurring influences. This profile connects his official Instagram archive with the new Los Angeles city page and the international visual gallery on UrbanArtsNews.com. It also places his work within searches for Op Art, optical murals, immersive installations and contemporary artists working with spatial illusion.
