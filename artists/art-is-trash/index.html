About Art Is Trash

Art Is Trash is known for transforming discarded materials and everyday urban spaces into temporary works of street art. The artist's interventions often appear directly in the streets, creating spontaneous encounters between art, public space and the city.

Barcelona has played an important role in the visual identity of Art Is Trash, with works appearing in urban environments and becoming part of the city's constantly changing street-art landscape.

Urban Arts News presents selected Instagram posts and street-art discoveries connected to Art Is Trash, Barcelona and contemporary urban culture.
