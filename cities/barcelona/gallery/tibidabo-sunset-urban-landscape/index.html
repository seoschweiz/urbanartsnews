Tibidabo Sunset Above Barcelona

The Temple of the Sacred Heart stands above Barcelona as sunset colours spread across the mountains and Mediterranean coast. From Tibidabo, the city appears as a vast connected landscape shaped by architecture, mobility and creative communities. At street level, urban artists turn sections of that landscape into personal, political and shared visual narratives.

Barcelona Urban Art News presents this photograph as part of its visual documentation of Urban Art Barcelona, city culture and the changing metropolitan landscape.
