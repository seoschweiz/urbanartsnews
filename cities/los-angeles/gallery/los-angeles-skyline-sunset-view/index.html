Panoramic Los Angeles Skyline Beneath a Pastel Sunset

A broad panorama stretches from the rugged foreground hills across the Los Angeles basin to the distant Downtown skyline. Soft pink clouds and warm evening light give the dense urban landscape an atmospheric, almost dreamlike appearance. Seen at this scale, Los Angeles becomes a vast canvas in which urban art connects architecture, communities and public space.

Los Angeles Urban Art News presents this photograph as part of its visual documentation of Urban Art Los Angeles, city culture and the changing metropolitan landscape.
