Badalona Coastal Tower and Mediterranean Viewpoint

A stone coastal structure and tall communications tower overlook Badalona’s rocky Mediterranean shoreline. Strong horizontal lines in the sea wall meet the vertical landmark beneath a vivid blue sky. Urban art often responds to such infrastructure by revealing overlooked histories and creating new ways to experience the city’s edges.
