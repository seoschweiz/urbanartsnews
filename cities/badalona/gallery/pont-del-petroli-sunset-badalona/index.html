Pont del Petroli Pier at Sunset in Badalona

The Pont del Petroli extends across calm water toward the setting sun on the Badalona coast. Golden light transforms the industrial pier into a quiet visual landmark above the Mediterranean. Its reinvention as public space mirrors urban art’s ability to give infrastructure new cultural meaning.

Badalona Urban Art News presents this photograph as part of its visual documentation of Urban Art Badalona, city culture and the changing metropolitan landscape.
