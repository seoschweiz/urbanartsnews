Badalona Beach and the Blue Mediterranean Sea

A broad sandy beach curves along Badalona’s Mediterranean coastline beneath a clear blue sky. The open shoreline and distant city structures reveal the close relationship between recreation, nature and metropolitan life. Urban art brings another layer of identity to this coastal environment through murals, public interventions and creative community spaces.
