Barcelona Featured Artist

Art Is Trash

Art Is Trash creates ephemeral street art from discarded materials in Barcelona. His improvised figures transform overlooked objects into expressive characters with humour and social commentary. The temporary nature of the work reflects the rapid rhythm of public space and everyday city life. His practice has become closely associated with Barcelona’s independent and unconventional urban-art identity.
